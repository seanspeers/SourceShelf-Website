Common questions

What to know before you begin

Does SourceShelf save only full webpages?

No. You can capture the full page, detected main content, selected text, a visible area, or multiple research highlights.

Can I review a capture before saving it?

Yes. A recipe can place captures in a local review queue so you can edit the Markdown, destination, notes, and custom fields first.

Does SourceShelf upload captured pages?

No. Safari captures are processed and saved locally.

Can captures be combined with PDFs and Office documents?

Yes. Safari captures and converted files coexist in the Library and can be added to the same saved pack.
