Bring scattered knowledge together

Useful information rarely lives in one place. It is spread across reports, PDFs, slide decks, spreadsheets, scanned pages, notes, and browser tabs. SourceShelf brings those materials into a searchable local Library while preserving where each source came from.

Save a useful article from Safari, convert a folder of project documents, or use local OCR on a scanned PDF. SourceShelf turns the result into structured Markdown that remains accessible outside the app.

Use the AI you choose

SourceShelf is not another AI chatbot and does not lock your knowledge to one model. A compatible AI client on your Mac can connect to a saved pack through read-only Model Context Protocol access.

The client may use a model running locally on your Mac or a cloud-backed model. SourceShelf itself does not upload the pack or call an AI service.

  • Understand read-only MCP knowledge access →

Only the pack you approve

Each Local AI authorization creates an immutable snapshot containing only the saved pack you selected. AI clients can search that snapshot and read bounded passages, but they cannot change your sources or browse arbitrary files.

Original file paths, security bookmarks, output-folder locations, and unselected Library items are not included. Stop sharing the pack at any time to invalidate the connection.

Open files, not a closed vault

SourceShelf's generated sources are ordinary Markdown files. Open them in your preferred editor, make changes, and return to SourceShelf when you are ready.

Refresh & Compare shows what was added, changed, removed, or left unchanged before you update an export or shared AI snapshot.

Common questions

What to know before you begin

Does SourceShelf include an AI model?

No. SourceShelf prepares and organizes knowledge for compatible AI applications. You remain free to choose the local or cloud-backed model you use.

Does SourceShelf upload my documents?

No. SourceShelf performs its own capture, conversion, OCR, organization, and export work locally on your Mac.

Can a smaller local model use a SourceShelf pack?

Yes, when the model is used through a compatible AI client. SourceShelf provides focused search results and bounded source passages so the client does not need to load every document at once.

Can an AI client edit or delete my sources?

No. SourceShelf's MCP access is read-only and limited to the immutable snapshot of the pack you authorize.
