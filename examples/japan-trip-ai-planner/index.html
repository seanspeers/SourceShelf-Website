Product evidence

Ask questions using your own sources

These are actual responses from the demonstration—not fabricated interface mockups. The compatible AI app searched the selected SourceShelf pack and answered from its travel research.

Question 01

“Create a 10-day itinerary based on my preferences”

The answer combines the trip’s pace, cultural priorities, public-transit preference, budget, and saved destination research into one coherent route.

Actual demo response · SourceShelf pack connected read-only
Question 02

“Which Tokyo neighborhood should we stay in?”

The recommendation selects Ueno after weighing the saved neighborhood guide against the travelers’ preference for culture and a balanced pace.

Actual demo response · Sources consulted through the pack
Question 03

“Compare our travel options”

The answer compares Tokyo neighborhoods and Kyoto versus Okinawa using the saved goals, logistics, weather tradeoffs, and fictional budget.
