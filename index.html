Your knowledge remains yours

Private by design.

PDF extraction, local OCR, Safari capture, Library work, and Ask This Pack all run on your device. Ask This Pack uses Apple’s on-device model on supported Apple Intelligence devices.

SourceShelf does not upload your documents, requires no SourceShelf cloud account, and sends no app telemetry or tracking. A third-party AI client connected through MCP follows its own model and privacy policies.
